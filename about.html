About Me

Hi, I’m Shangyi

I’m a Music Technology master’s student with a concentration in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University. In summer 2026 I interned at Lilith Games as a Software Engineer (Audio), building interactive audio and haptic systems for mobile games. My previous experience includes a sound design internship at Lilith on AFK Journey, working as a Technical Sound Designer at Tencent, and a Game Programmer role at Toyz Electronics, where I worked on game backend systems and audio implementation.

My music training started with classical piano at the age of 7, and I loved how music could be a mesmerizing world filled with creativity. I competed in many piano performance competitions during my teenage years, winning third prize in the Steinway Piano Competition (junior group, South China region, 2015) and an honorable mention in the Chopin International Piano Competition in Hartford, 2019.

I later started doing more compositional work for games and have fallen for game audio ever since. I want to keep using my background in music and technology to serve story and narration in games, films, and documentaries.
